Privacy policy

Your data, short

Hunchline has no ads, no tracking and no analytics. We only keep what the game needs to work.

What we process, and why

In the app.

  • A random account ID. Created when you first open the app, so you can play without signing up.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional). If you link it, Apple gives us an identifier and, if you choose to share it, your email address or Apple's private relay address. We also keep the token Apple issues, only so we can revoke Hunchline's access to your Apple ID when you delete your account.
  • Your display name and invite code. The name is up to 20 characters and you choose it. Your friend sees it, and so does anyone who opens an invite link containing your code.
  • Your guesses with their time, and which days you played.
  • The jar. If you play against a friend: the link between your two accounts, each side's running tally for the month, and the result of past months. The jar is virtual. No money is collected, held or paid by us.
  • Push notifications (optional). If you allow them, your device's notification token, so we can tell you when your friend has played. You can turn this off in the app or in iOS Settings.
  • Plus subscription. Whether you have Plus and when it expires. Apple handles the payment. We never see your card or Apple ID password.

We use this to run the game and its features (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Notifications rely on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).

On this website. The website sets no cookies and uses no trackers. It remembers your US/Metric choice only while the tab is open. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) receives your IP address when you load a page. On an invite page, your IP address and the invite code also go to our database provider so the page can show who invited you. Both are needed to deliver the pages and keep them secure (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Daily questions. They are prepared from public news with the help of an AI service. No personal data is sent to it.

Who receives data

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and server functions. Our project runs in the EU (Ireland).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications and payments.
  • GitHub: hosts the website (USA).

These providers process data on our behalf or, in Apple's case, under their own terms. Where data goes to the USA, this relies on safeguards required by the GDPR, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. We do not sell data and do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. You can do that in the app: open the More tab and tap Delete account. Your account and everything linked to it (guesses, stats, display name, jar) are deleted straight away, and if you linked Sign in with Apple, Hunchline's access to your Apple ID is revoked. If you have a jar friend, the jar ends for them too. You can also write to contact@hunchline.online and we delete it within 30 days. Deleting your account doesn't cancel Plus; cancel it in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Past jar months are kept only as a result per month, and are removed with the account. The hosting provider keeps its own server logs under its own policy.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy in a portable format, and you can object to processing. You can withdraw consent at any time. Write to contact@hunchline.online.

You can also complain to the Austrian data protection authority: Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, www.dsb.gv.at. You may complain to the authority in your own country as well.

Children

Hunchline is rated 13+ on the App Store. In Austria, if you are under 14, please ask a parent or guardian before you use it.

Changes

If we change how we handle data, we update this page and its date. For material changes we also tell you in the app.
