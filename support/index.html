Common questions

Is the jar real money?

No. The jar is a virtual tally between you and your friend. Nothing is paid, collected or held in the app. The one who is further off at the end of the month treats the other to a drink, a movie or anything else. It resets on the 1st of every month.

How do I play with a friend?

Open the Jar tab, share your invite link or code, and your friend enters it. Each of you plays the same daily question.

Where do I manage or cancel Plus?

Apple handles subscriptions. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I turn notifications off?

In the iPhone Settings app, open Notifications, then Hunchline.

A question or answer looks wrong.

Email us the question and a link to a source. We check every report.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email us from any address and say you want your account deleted. We delete it and everything linked to it within 30 days.
